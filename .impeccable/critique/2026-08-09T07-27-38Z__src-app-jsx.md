## Design Specificity Verdict

Authored-for-this-product, with a category-interchangeable middle third.

Authored: RoadmapGenerator (lakh-denominated deterministic scoping instrument, no email gate), Work section's refusal to fake case studies ("we won't invent them", "Run Lighthouse on this page"), signed Guarantee with named founders, ₹ bands aligned across pricing/roadmap/budget-select.

Interchangeable: Process section ("proven four-step", "dive deep", "battle-tested" — zero numbers), About values grid ("Ship Fast / Own It / Stay Sharp / Be Honest"), About opener ("deep engineering capability with sharp product thinking"), service adjectives ("blazing speed", "packets racing on secure highways").

Browser overlay skipped: no browser automation exposed.

## Overall Impression

Visual system is disciplined; the words are not. Site preaches "numbers as commitments, no performance" and mostly delivers (Guarantee, Work, Roadmap, public ₹ bands) — then undercuts itself with six invented KPI chips, triple-stated promises, quadruple-stated pricing caveat, an 11-item FAQ wall between the ask and the form, and two sections of pure agency filler. Biggest opportunity: delete ~30% of the words and every number Revora doesn't control; the remaining page would be more Zerodha than Zerodha.

## What's Working

1. **Guarantee as signed document** — numbers as terms, not performance; deliberately un-animated; signed by named founders. Original trust pattern.
2. **Work section's structural honesty** — "we won't invent them", verifiable self-teardown, "Target:" not "Result:".
3. **RoadmapGenerator** — un-gated, deterministic, printable, price-story consistent site-wide. The one artifact no competitor has.

## Questions to Consider

1. Guarantee doctrine says "a number that is a commitment should not perform" — why are six invented numbers performing one section above? What if every number Revora doesn't control were deleted tonight?
2. The roadmap generator already contains the services, prices, and process. Could the homepage be generator + Guarantee + form, and nothing else?
3. Zerodha states a fact once and lets silence persuade. Revora states its promises 3× and its caveat 4×. Serving the visitor's memory — or the founders' anxiety?
